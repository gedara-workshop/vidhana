What this cannot tell you
The IRD listing omits gazettes from inside its own date range — seven were recovered from the Internet Archive and are marked recovered. A gazette nobody indexes, which rescinds one we hold, would be invisible here. Where a rule’s history has a hole, results say so. Summaries are machine-written; the gazette is the source of truth.
