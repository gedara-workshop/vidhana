The qualifier system

A result can carry five caveats at once. Five badges is a wall nobody reads — and the one that matters most gets lost in it.

Two moves. First, state before sentence — the legal state is a pill you recognise at a glance, and only the consequence gets words. Second, each caveat attaches to the claim it weakens: a missing gazette qualifies the standing claim; low confidence qualifies only the summary text; provenance qualifies the document's identity. Scoped this way they stop competing, because they are no longer four things saying “careful” in the same place.
