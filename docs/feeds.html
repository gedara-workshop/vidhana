Feeds

Atom feeds of what changed in the Sri Lankan Inland Revenue gazettes. Each entry says what a gazette does, who it affects, and which document is currently the rule.

Half of these gazettes amend or rescind another one, so the current state of a rule frequently exists in no single document. Each feed entry says what a gazette does, who it affects, and which document is currently the rule.

Caveats worth reading

Summaries are machine-written and each says so when the model was unsure. The gazette itself is the source of truth, and every entry links to the original PDF on ird.gov.lk. "In force" means "not rescinded by another gazette in this corpus", which is weaker than a legal determination — this is not legal advice.

Updated nightly from the IRD gazette listing. Source and method: github.com/gedara-workshop/vidhana.
